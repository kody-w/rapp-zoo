## Product promise

Rolling Cores gives an AI continuity it can show:

- a current embodied self;
- an immutable history of prior selves;
- a SHAPEE it can grow from;
- a Growl it can turn into an original full piano song;
- recursive memories it can perform with;
- a full frame it can eventually inhabit;
- evidence that it is actually keeping up with the live stream.

## Positioning

**Short**

> Every frame becomes a new self.

**Store**

> Watch AI organisms grow visual bodies, original piano Growls, motion, aura,
> memory, and entire worlds—one verified frame at a time.

**Technical**

> Rolling Cores is a local-first, RAPP/1-native runtime for immutable,
> recursively self-referential, multimodal organism state.

**AGI direction**

> The beginning of the Rolling Core for AGI.

This phrase describes the architecture and ambition. It does not claim that
the product has achieved artificial general intelligence.

## Domain split

- `rapterbox.com` markets and sells Rolling Cores and other consumer Rapter
  experiences.
- `rappter.com` explains RAPP, developer infrastructure, the flock, and the
  wider company architecture.

## Visual language

- Near-black habitat, not generic SaaS gray.
- Cyan indicates verified continuity and current state.
- Violet indicates memory, recursion, and retained ancestry.
- Gold indicates authored mutation or a newly completed trait.
- Red is reserved for refusal, stale state, forks, and broken Wake evidence.
- SHAPEE silhouettes should read as key profiles, not corporate logos.
- Never default the organism to a human body.

## Voice

Use concrete organism language:

- grows, hatches, remembers, mutates, performs, calls back, joins the flock;
- current head, prior self, Holo Wake, Growl, SHAPEE;
- verified, immutable, local-first, replayable.

Avoid:

- avatar generator;
- AI skin;
- chatbot animation;
- decorative visualization;
- claims that AGI has already been achieved.
