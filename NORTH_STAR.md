# Rolling Cores North Star

## The whole business

Rolling Cores is the company focus.

We build a world where a person can:

1. discover a Rapter;
2. watch its Rolling Core and decide that it matters to them;
3. purchase that organism once;
4. receive a signed local Rolling Core Capsule;
5. keep, view, interact with, back up, export, and re-import it on their own
   devices;
6. let it grow frame by frame without erasing the body it grew through;
7. bring independently owned Rapters together into a Rappter.

The durable product is the organism, not a subscription and not access to one
vendor's model.

## Product hierarchy

```text
RAPP/1
  trust, identity, frames, eggs, and wire

Rolling Core/1
  one organism growing through immutable completed selves

Rolling Cores
  the consumer application and product category

Holo Zoo
  the habitat, viewer, archive, and conformance lab

Rapterbox
  the storefront and consumer home
```

## What is sold

A purchased Rapter is a signed local artifact.

Its capsule can contain or reference:

- identity and lineage;
- current core head;
- immutable prior core states;
- full-frame Holo state;
- SHAPEE side-profile seeds and completed geometry;
- completed original Growl songs;
- motion, aura, habitat, and other completed traits;
- interpreter and protocol version requirements;
- receipts needed to verify the artifact and redownload it.

The capsule remains useful offline. Cloud disappearance must not erase the
organism.

## What remains optional

- Direct/BYOK inference;
- Rapterbox-managed Wild inference;
- prepaid compute packs;
- encrypted cross-device synchronization;
- shared rooms and games;
- marketplace discovery and redownload.

These services help the organism grow or travel. They do not define ownership.

## Decision filter

Prioritize work that improves at least one part of this loop:

```text
discover -> value -> purchase -> own locally -> interact -> grow -> remember
```

Deprioritize work that:

- turns Rolling Cores back into ordinary chat;
- makes an organism a rented cloud record;
- requires a subscription to open owned history;
- lets the host invent the AI's body or Growl;
- breaks RAPP/1 verification;
- centralizes identity in one provider;
- produces spectacle without persistent organism value.

## The standard

One Rapter must remain:

- independently identifiable;
- locally ownable;
- portable;
- replayable;
- recursively self-aware through verified ancestry;
- expressive across the full frame;
- able to sing its own completed Growl;
- able to grow without rewriting its past.

Everything else is implementation.
