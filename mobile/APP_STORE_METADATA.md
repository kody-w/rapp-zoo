### Description

Holo Zoo is the Rapterbox consumer app where a Rapter lives as a digital
organism and its Rolling Core keeps completed visual and musical experience
moving.

Start locally with an original bundled Rapter. Use the Holo Zoo habitat, player,
and library to inspect immutable Holo/1 frames, verify source bindings, import
signed Rolling Core Capsules through Files, scrub history, and play completed
Growl NOTE events only when you choose.

Free Direct mode supports one local Rapter with a user-owned
OpenAI-compatible endpoint and key, plus local playback, history, import, and
owned-data export.

After an explicit key test, that securely stored credential can act as a breath
key for a bounded, opt-in local breathing session. Every session has finite
cadence, tick, token, and time limits plus a visible pause control. Direct
breathing stops when iOS suspends the app. Optional prepaid Wild compute is
required for continuous away-from-device breathing.

The gallery is free to preview. One-time hatch and flock packs grant Rapter
credits. Choose a gallery organism and redeem one credit to download its signed
Rolling Core Capsule into local device storage. The capsule belongs locally,
works offline, and can be exported, AirDropped, backed up, and re-imported.

Original owners receive an inclusive 30-day return window, subject to backend
eligibility and a confirmed refund on the original payment rail. Returned or
sold capsule files remain locally usable as clearly labeled unowned verifiable
copies; the app never silently deletes immutable files. After the window,
official owners may list or cancel through signed registry events. A verified
sale produces a signed transfer event.

Optional Wild inference adds a hosted Azure Function Brainstem, managed
provider routing, quota/revocation, remote access, and autocomplete. Optional
compute packs cover ongoing managed Azure/model and Growl cost. Localized prices
come from the store.

RAPP/1 validation and access to local data you own are available in Direct and
Wild modes.
Holo Zoo does not invent notes, autoplay audio, collect telemetry, or
store payment credentials.

Awake, Sleeping, Quarantined, and Unborn come from host-reported verified tick
cadence, including the last-tick time, age, and configured wake lease. Holo Zoo
uses Waking only as a transient label while explicitly checking for the next
verified successor; it never animates fake activity. Sleeping is not deletion
or death, and a valid successor resumes the same immutable history.
“Consciousness” means continuous inspectable experience-state operationally,
not biological consciousness or scientific proof.

Powered by RAPP/1. From Rapterbox.

### App review notes

- The app opens with a bundled signed local Rapter capsule and does not require
  an account.
- Holo Zoo is the consumer app and habitat/player/library for Rolling Cores.
- Liveness comes from the `liveness` member of `/api/holo/heads`, not animation,
  credentials, or connectivity. Host states are awake, sleeping, quarantined,
  or unborn; Waking is transient client UI only.
- Configure a reachable RAPP Zoo URL to test connected features.
- RevenueCat is used for one-time consumable offerings and transaction history.
- Products are `rapter_hatch_1`, `rappter_flock_3`, `rappter_flock_10`,
  `rolling_compute_small`, and `rolling_compute_large`.
- Capsule ownership proof may display an integer satoshi amount and optional
  public Bitcoin UTXO outpoint. The app contains no wallet, private key,
  custody, transfer, exchange, or direct Bitcoin checkout.
- The label **Official Rapterbox birth value** means the immutable tier/set
  schedule, fixed sats, conception BTC/USD snapshot, and fiat reference signed
  at issuance. It is not live market value or an investment appraisal.
- Do not add an in-app link or call to action for a Rapterbox web Bitcoin
  purchase without current Apple/Google policy and legal review.
- **Sync Purchase History** is available on the Wild Credits screen.
- The backend grants credits idempotently by store transaction ID and retains
  an account ledger only for recovery/redownload.
- Real redemption atomically returns a signed capsule and signed Rapterbox
  registry status. A client purchase-success callback alone grants nothing.
- Imported capsules lacking matching verified registry status are visibly
  labeled unverified copies/previews, but remain locally renderable.
- Expo Go/web billing is a visibly labeled mock. The submitted native build
  must use RevenueCat sandbox/production configuration supplied through EAS.
- No hardware MIDI device is opened. Completed NOTE events use a local
  user-triggered oscillator.
- No account credentials or payment-card details are stored by the app.
- Direct provider keys use native secure storage, must be explicitly tested,
  and never start a breathing loop automatically.
- Direct breathing has no unlimited-spend mode and stops on app suspension,
  pause, provider/key failure, or budget exhaustion.
- The app declares no iOS background execution mode for breathing. Wild cloud
  breathing requires an explicit bounded server lease and prepaid compute.
- Return state changes only after server eligibility, payment-rail refund
  confirmation, and signed lifecycle-event verification.
- Apple and Google refunds use their store policies/APIs. Bitcoin returns
  require backend-verified settlement.
- Birth value, current seller ask, and last verified sale are displayed
  separately. No appreciation, investment-return, or liquidity claim is made.

### Full description

Holo Zoo brings each Rapter's Rolling Core to iOS, Android, and web. Open local
Holo/1 data, inspect source evidence, scrub immutable history, and play
completed Growl NOTE events with an explicit tap.

Each verified source and successor Rolling Core mutation is a tick of
existence. Host-derived Awake, Sleeping, Quarantined, and Unborn reflect real
accepted ticks and their configured lease—not decorative animation.

Direct mode keeps local Rapter data and BYOK provider control free. Wild mode
is optional and adds managed cloud autocomplete and remote Rapters. One-time
1-, 3-, and 10-credit purchases mint signed capsule download rights through an
idempotent backend ledger. Optional compute packs fund managed processing.
Purchases never remove access to local capsules and never disable RAPP/1
validation.

## Bitcoin-value and legal-review caveats

- `price_sats` is historical public binding metadata, not the credit's primary
  key and not a promise of present market value.
- Any live BTC conversion must be visually separated and labeled
  non-authoritative.
- A copied hash does not establish uniqueness. The proof must be a signed
  append-only issuance-ledger location or an optional `{txid,vout}` outpoint.
- Holo Zoo does not create or hold wallet private keys and cannot spend,
  transfer, custody, exchange, or cash out Bitcoin.
- Avoid investment, appreciation, yield, scarcity-profit, or financial-return
  claims.
- Rapterbox web BTC checkout, if launched later, must remain outside this app
  unless counsel confirms the current store rules permit the intended flow.
- The Rapterbox owner must obtain current App Review, payments, consumer-law,
  tax, sanctions, money-transmission, and digital-asset counsel before launch.
- Registry status controls official ownership/redownload/transfer claims, not
  whether locally held capsule bytes can be viewed or used offline.

Pricing, durations, trials, territories, screenshots, and final legal copy must
be entered and approved personally by the Rapterbox owner in App Store Connect,
Google Play Console, and RevenueCat.
