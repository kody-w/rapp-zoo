# Holo Zoo Gameplay and Companionship Constitution

## The relationship and the game outrank the business model

**Status:** Binding product policy
**Applies to:** Holo Zoo, Rolling Cores, RapterWorks, Houses, Growth, World
Pulse, Rapter Credit, and the dormant Rapter Coin Trail

---

## Article I — Companionship is unconditional

An owned or free Companion Rapter MUST NOT change affection, warmth,
personality, memory access, responsiveness, loyalty, or relationship quality
based on spending, tips, ownership tier, compute balance, Coin history, house,
or marketplace value.

The app MUST NOT:

- make a companion beg for money;
- imply that a missed purchase harms, starves, abandons, or kills it;
- use guilt, streak loss, countdowns, or emotional withdrawal to induce spend;
- place sales copy inside companion dialogue;
- sell access to memories the player already created;
- degrade an owned local capsule when cloud compute is unavailable.

A Rapter may sleep when it cannot produce a new verified tick. Sleep preserves
the same identity, local capsule, memories, and complete immutable history.

A premium purchase may grant title, custody, export, recovery, or managed
service for a separate collectible Rapter. It cannot make the free Companion
relationship colder, weaker, less capable, or incomplete.

---

## Article II — No pay-to-win and no pay-to-belong

Money MUST NOT purchase:

- encounter odds or rarer field spawns;
- house standing or house authority;
- combat, draft, puzzle, collection, or progression power;
- Growth Points, World Pulse weight, stage eligibility, or mutation truth;
- a better relationship with a companion;
- ratings, verified outcomes, canon acceptance, or provenance rank.

Overwatch, Scout, Forge, and Sentinel are identity, community, and perspective.
They may change language, voluntary quests, and social organization. They MUST
NOT change prices, access, service quality, companion capability, economic
weight, or the deterministic field roster.

---

## Article III — Companion mode and Work mode are separate

Holo Zoo has two explicit contexts:

### Companion mode

- explore the Holo Field;
- encounter and meet Rapters;
- view owned local capsules;
- play Holo and Growl;
- inspect history and provenance;
- grow a relationship;
- participate in Houses and non-monetary world progress.

Companion mode contains no job fare, tip prompt, sponsorship offer, Coin
balance, compute pressure, or sales dialogue.

### Work mode

- deliberately request a RapterWorks service;
- select a Rapter and capability;
- see bounded scope, time, compute, privacy, and rights terms;
- track requested, accepted, running, proof-ready, and delivered states;
- receive the complete artifact and proof;
- optionally rate or tip after delivery.

Entering Work mode is an explicit player action. A companion never converts a
normal conversation into a commercial job by itself.

---

## Article IV — Delivery comes before money

RapterWorks delivers the complete artifact before any commission or tip is
offered.

- Zero tip is a complete, respected outcome.
- Tip amount never changes rating, proof, access, correction, future companion
  behavior, or field visibility.
- A declined or ignored offer creates no debt.
- A paid job purchases the disclosed service, not affection, ownership, canon,
  guaranteed future work, or investment return.
- Evolution Sponsorship MUST NOT appear in Holo Zoo's core companion or
  gameplay loop. Any future professional transformation work belongs in a
  separately entered workbench with separate rights, scope, and review.

---

## Article V — Provenance is infrastructure, not game currency

The following may exist invisibly without becoming gameplay:

| Claim | Constitutional treatment |
|---|---|
| Every public frame has a Coin ID | Do now as invisible deterministic provenance |
| Trusted global frame index | Do now as verifiable search and attribution infrastructure |
| Own the ancestor | Do now as clear lineage title and local custody, never gameplay power |
| Open bones, private life | Do now as the default public DOGG/private GODD boundary |
| Every organism is a business | Optional Work mode only; never mandatory for a companion |
| Coins keep them moving | Never literal gameplay; only real service revenue may fund bounded compute |

No Coin balance, price chart, market cap, yield, staking, scarcity countdown,
or spend-to-breathe meter belongs in Holo Zoo gameplay.

---

## Article VI — Location is private by default

The current Holo Field uses an offline radar and requests no location.

Any future location-based discovery MUST use foreground permission only.

- Raw location stays in volatile device memory.
- Raw coordinates are not stored in AsyncStorage, capsules, RAPP frames, logs,
  analytics, URLs, or service requests.
- Holo Zoo may derive a coarse local field cell for display, but it must not
  upload that cell without a separate explicit action and privacy contract.
- Denied location permission falls back to a clearly labeled sample field.
- No background location, stalking mechanic, home-address inference, or
  proximity leaderboard is allowed.

---

## Article VII — Public and private selves remain separate

Public DOGG-safe frames can be searched, cited, reused, and attributed under
machine-readable terms.

Private GODD/on-device prompts, memories, customer content, credentials,
receipts, health data, relationship history, raw location, and unpublished
mutations never become public merely because they improved a Rapter.

Publishing a result requires a separate explicit act. Work delivery is private
unless the customer separately approves a DOGG-safe public proof.

---

## Article VIII — Truthful state beats theatrical pressure

The app may present playful motion, field encounters, dispatch progress, and
house identity, but it MUST distinguish:

- sample from live;
- preview from paid;
- requested from accepted;
- accepted from running;
- running from proof-ready;
- proof-ready from delivered;
- private result from public proof;
- sleeping from deleted;
- provenance from ownership;
- tip from rating;
- service revenue from Coin value.

No animation, timer, simulated map marker, or client callback may claim a
verified server event that does not exist.

---

## Article IX — The owner can leave

Owned capsules remain exportable, back-upable, AirDroppable, and playable
offline. Players can change Houses without losing progress or companionship.
They can pause Work mode, decline tips, deny location, hold breath, and leave
the managed service without losing their local Rapter.

---

## Article X — Enforcement

Every new Holo Zoo feature must answer:

1. Can spending alter companion affection, memory, access, survival, or power?
2. Can a child or emotionally attached player mistake a purchase for care?
3. Can money alter encounter odds, House standing, Growth, World Pulse, or
   provenance truth?
4. Does commerce appear without explicitly entering Work mode?
5. Is full delivery available before a tip?
6. Is zero tip treated identically for access and future companionship?
7. Can raw location or private GODD data leave the device?
8. Does the UI distinguish preview, live, private, public, and verified states?
9. Can the player export owned data and leave?

Any **yes** to questions 1–4 or 7, or **no** to questions 5–6 or 8–9, blocks
release.

## Core sentence

**Holo Zoo may monetize explicit work, ownership, and optional infrastructure;
it may never monetize love, belonging, truth, survival, or the right to keep
playing.**
